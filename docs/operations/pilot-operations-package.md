# Pilot Operations Package

This guide supports controlled pilot onboarding for Loyalty Card UAE.

## Pilot Onboarding Guide

1. Confirm pilot business contact.
2. Confirm business name, branches, and owner email.
3. Create the business as System Administrator.
4. Assign trial or active subscription.
5. Configure branding.
6. Create Business Owner account.
7. Confirm Business Owner can log in.
8. Create Branch Manager and Staff users.
9. Create loyalty program.
10. Enroll first customer.
11. Scan customer QR.
12. Issue first stamp.
13. Test reward-ready flow.
14. Review alerts.
15. Prepare a sample message.

## Business Owner Onboarding Guide

Business Owner should learn:

- Dashboard overview.
- Branch management.
- Staff management.
- Customer enrollment.
- Loyalty program setup.
- Customer profile.
- Notifications and alerts.
- Reward redemption.
- Message preparation.
- CSV exports.

## Staff Onboarding Guide

Staff should learn:

- How to log in.
- How to enroll a customer.
- How to open scanner.
- How to scan a customer card.
- How to issue one stamp.
- When a reason is required for multiple stamps.
- What to do if QR is invalid.
- Why reward redemption may be restricted.

## Support Process

Pilot support workflow:

1. Business reports issue.
2. Support owner records business, user, role, device, and page.
3. Reproduce issue in development data if possible.
4. Check Audit Center and platform health.
5. Determine severity.
6. Communicate workaround or fix timeline.
7. Close issue only after pilot user confirms.

## Issue Escalation Process

Severity levels:

- Critical: login down, database down, scan/stamp unavailable for all users.
- High: one business cannot complete scanner or stamp workflow.
- Medium: confusing UX or role-specific issue.
- Low: text, layout, or training issue.

Escalation:

1. Support owner investigates.
2. Technical owner reviews logs and audit events.
3. System Administrator communicates status to pilot business.
4. Fix is validated in development before release.

## Pilot Success Checklist

Pilot is successful when:

- Business Owner can operate without developer assistance.
- Staff can scan and issue stamps on mobile.
- Customers can open their card.
- At least one reward is redeemed.
- Alerts are understandable.
- Message preparation is clear.
- No cross-business data exposure occurs.
- Pilot business confirms workflow value.
