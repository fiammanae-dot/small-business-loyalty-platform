# Incident Classification Matrix

Use this matrix during pilot operations.

## P1 Critical

Definition:

Platform is unavailable or a security/data isolation issue is suspected.

Examples:

- App down.
- Database down.
- Cross-business data visible.
- Staff can access System Administrator pages.
- Stamp or reward data corrupted.

Response Target:

Immediate.

Escalation Path:

Platform support -> Technical owner -> System Administrator.

Resolution Target:

Same day or rollback.

## P2 High

Definition:

Major workflow blocked for a pilot business.

Examples:

- Staff cannot scan.
- Business Owner cannot log in.
- Customers cannot open cards.
- Reward redemption blocked for all managers.

Response Target:

30 minutes during pilot hours.

Escalation Path:

Business Owner -> Platform support -> Technical owner.

Resolution Target:

Same business day.

## P3 Medium

Definition:

Workflow works with workaround but causes friction.

Examples:

- Camera permission confusing.
- Mobile layout awkward.
- Alert workflow unclear.
- Message preparation misunderstood.

Response Target:

Same business day.

Escalation Path:

Branch Manager or Business Owner -> Platform support.

Resolution Target:

Within pilot sprint.

## P4 Low

Definition:

Minor UI, wording, or training issue.

Examples:

- Label unclear.
- Cosmetic spacing issue.
- Help text missing.

Response Target:

Two business days.

Escalation Path:

Support log.

Resolution Target:

Next planned polish cycle.
