# Pilot Onboarding Guide

This guide explains how to run a controlled Loyalty Card UAE pilot with selected businesses.

## Purpose Of Pilot

The pilot validates that Loyalty Card UAE works in real business operations before commercial launch.

Pilot goals:

- Confirm business onboarding works.
- Confirm staff can scan and issue stamps.
- Confirm customers can open cards.
- Confirm rewards can be redeemed.
- Confirm referrals are understandable.
- Confirm support process works.
- Identify UX and operational gaps.

## Pilot Timeline

Recommended pilot structure:

- Week 0: setup and training.
- Week 1: supervised launch.
- Week 2: daily operational use.
- Week 3: issue resolution and workflow refinement.
- Week 4: pilot review and go/no-go decision.

## Pilot Expectations

Platform team:

- Provide setup.
- Provide training.
- Monitor health.
- Respond to support issues.
- Collect feedback.

Pilot business:

- Use the system in real daily operations.
- Train selected staff.
- Report issues quickly.
- Avoid manual workarounds unless agreed.
- Participate in pilot review.

## Pilot Responsibilities

System Administrator:

- Create business.
- Configure subscription.
- Verify accounts.
- Monitor Audit Center, Billing Center, Tenant Center, and Health Analytics.

Business Owner:

- Create branches.
- Create staff.
- Create loyalty programs.
- Review alerts and messages.
- Confirm operational readiness.

Branch Manager:

- Validate scanner.
- Support branch staff.
- Review daily branch activity.

Staff:

- Enroll customers.
- Scan customer cards.
- Issue stamps.
- Explain cards and referrals to customers.

## Success Criteria

Pilot is successful when:

- At least one business completes setup.
- Staff can scan and issue stamps without developer help.
- Customers can open cards on mobile.
- At least one reward reaches Reward Ready.
- At least one reward is redeemed.
- Referral sharing is understood.
- Alerts are understandable.
- Support issues are tracked and closed.

## Communication Process

Recommended channels:

- One business contact.
- One platform support contact.
- Daily check-in during first week.
- Weekly review after launch.

Record:

- Issue summary.
- Affected role.
- Device/browser.
- Priority.
- Status.

## Issue Reporting Process

When reporting an issue, collect:

- Business name.
- User role.
- Page or action.
- Device and browser.
- Steps to reproduce.
- Screenshot if possible.
- Expected behavior.
- Actual behavior.

## Pilot Completion Process

At the end of pilot:

1. Review scorecard.
2. Review support tickets.
3. Review incidents.
4. Review customer/staff feedback.
5. List required fixes.
6. Decide go/no-go.
7. Document next phase.
