# Branch Manager Operations Guide

This guide supports daily branch operations during pilot.

## Daily Startup

At start of day:

1. Login.
2. Confirm branch dashboard loads.
3. Confirm correct branch name.
4. Open scanner.
5. Test camera permission or manual token entry.
6. Confirm staff know how to issue stamps.

## Scanner Checks

Verify:

- Scanner page opens.
- Camera starts if permission is granted.
- Manual token entry is visible.
- Valid QR opens scan result.
- Invalid QR shows safe error.
- Wrong-business QR is blocked.

## Customer Support

Help customers with:

- Opening customer card.
- Understanding stamp progress.
- Showing QR to staff.
- Sharing referral link.
- Understanding reward readiness.

Do not expose internal customer records to other customers.

## Program Management

Branch Manager can:

- View programs.
- View enrolled customers.
- Enroll customers into programs.

Branch Manager cannot:

- Create programs.
- Edit programs.
- Disable programs.

## Staff Oversight

Monitor:

- Staff can log in.
- Staff can scan cards.
- Staff can issue stamps.
- Staff understand multiple stamp reason requirement.
- Staff do not attempt restricted reward actions.

## Troubleshooting

Common issues:

- Camera denied: use manual token entry.
- QR invalid: confirm it is a Loyalty Card UAE card.
- Wrong business: ask customer for the correct business card.
- Disabled QR: contact Business Owner.
- Customer not found: enroll customer or check phone number.

## End-Of-Day Review

Review:

- Customers served.
- Stamps issued.
- Rewards redeemed.
- Alerts.
- Scanner issues.
- Staff feedback.

Report issues to Business Owner or support contact.
