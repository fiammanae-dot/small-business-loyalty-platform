# Staff Operations Guide

This guide helps Staff use Loyalty Card UAE during daily operations.

## Login

1. Open login page.
2. Enter staff email and password.
3. Confirm Staff dashboard opens.

If login fails, contact Branch Manager.

## Scanner Use

1. Open Staff -> Scanner.
2. Select Start Camera.
3. Allow camera permission.
4. Scan customer QR.

If camera does not work:

- Use Paste Token Manually.
- Ask customer to open their card clearly.
- Check screen brightness.

## Enroll Customer

1. Open Staff -> Add Customer.
2. Enter first name.
3. Enter phone number.
4. Submit.
5. Show customer the success page card link.

## Issue Stamp

1. Scan customer card.
2. Confirm customer and program.
3. Select stamp quantity.
4. Submit.

Rules:

- Use `+1 Stamp` for normal purchase.
- If quantity is more than 1, enter a reason.
- Do not issue stamps without a valid customer interaction.

## Reward Redemption

Staff cannot redeem rewards.

If customer is Reward Ready:

1. Notify Branch Manager or Business Owner.
2. Ask them to complete redemption.

## Customer Questions

Common answers:

- Customer card is opened through the public card link.
- Phone number is masked for privacy.
- QR is shown to staff to earn stamps.
- Referral link can be shared with friends.
- Rewards are redeemed by authorized users.

## Referral Handling

If customer asks about referrals:

1. Ask customer to open public card.
2. Use referral section.
3. Copy or share referral link.

Staff should not manually approve referrals.

## Common Issues

- Camera permission denied: use manual token entry.
- Invalid QR: ask customer to open correct card.
- Wrong business QR: customer is showing another business card.
- Multiple stamps blocked: enter reason.
- Reward Ready: contact manager.
