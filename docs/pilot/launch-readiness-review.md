# Launch Readiness Review

Use this review before the first real pilot.

## Platform Readiness

Assess:

- Authentication.
- Role permissions.
- Tenant isolation.
- Customer enrollment.
- Loyalty programs.
- Scanner.
- Stamp issuance.
- Reward redemption.
- Referrals.
- Engagement.
- Messages.
- Billing.
- Audit Center.
- Tenant Center.

Status:

- Ready
- Ready with notes
- Not ready

## Operational Readiness

Assess:

- Deployment runbook.
- Rollback runbook.
- Backups.
- Restore drill.
- Monitoring plan.
- Device QA.
- E2E test plan.

## Support Readiness

Assess:

- Support contact assigned.
- Escalation path defined.
- Incident matrix available.
- FAQ available.
- Findings log ready.
- Business training scheduled.

## Pilot Readiness

Assess:

- Pilot businesses selected.
- Accounts created.
- Training completed.
- Customer journey tested.
- Scanner tested on real devices.
- Support process tested.

## Commercial Readiness

Assess:

- Plans ready.
- Subscriptions ready.
- Manual invoices ready.
- Billing Center reviewed.
- Known limitations communicated.

## Known Limitations

- No automatic WhatsApp/SMS/email sending.
- No online payment gateway.
- Wallet integration is not live.
- Camera behavior depends on device/browser permissions.
- Custom domains are future-ready but not automated.

## Open Risks

- Real-device scanner behavior may vary.
- Pilot data must remain separate from development data.
- Support response must be manual during pilot.
- Backup restore should be practiced before first pilot.

## Recommended Actions Before First Pilot

1. Complete device QA.
2. Complete E2E journey testing.
3. Verify backup and restore drill.
4. Confirm pilot database and environment.
5. Train pilot business owner.
6. Train branch manager and staff.
7. Confirm support escalation path.
8. Set daily pilot check-in.
