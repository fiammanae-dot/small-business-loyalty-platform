# System Administrator Pilot Guide

This guide helps the System Administrator prepare and support selected pilot businesses in LoyaltyBase.

## Create a Business

1. Sign in as System Administrator.
2. Open Platform -> Tenant Overview.
3. Select Create Business.
4. Complete the setup wizard:
   - Business Details
   - Owner Account
   - First Branch
   - Subscription Plan
   - Branding
   - Review & Create
5. Submit the form and confirm the business appears in the business list.

## Assign a Plan

Plans are selected during business creation or edited later from:

Platform -> Tenant Overview -> Business -> Edit business

Available pilot plans include Starter, Growth, Multi-Branch, and Premium. Plan limits control branch and loyalty program creation.

## Create the Business Owner

The Business Owner account is created during business creation. Use a temporary password and share it securely with the pilot business contact.

The Business Owner should sign in and change operational setup through their dashboard.

## Create Branches

The first branch is created during business setup. Additional branches are created by the Business Owner from:

Business -> Branches

Branch limits are enforced by the assigned plan.

## Verify Subscription Status

Open:

Platform -> Subscriptions

Confirm:

- Business
- Current plan
- Status
- Renewal or expiry date

For active pilot use, subscription status should normally be Trial or Active.

## Enable Demo Mode

Open:

Platform -> Settings

Use Demo Mode to mark the environment as a demo or pilot workspace. Demo Mode adds a visible badge and reminds users that message delivery is manual only.

## Check Launch Readiness

Open:

Platform -> Settings -> Launch Readiness

Review pass/fail indicators for:

- Security
- Permissions
- Customers
- Programs
- Scanner
- Stamp Issuance
- Reward Redemption
- Engagement
- Messages
- Subscriptions

## Monitor Platform Health

Open:

Platform -> Health & Analytics

Use this page to monitor aggregate platform metrics without exposing customer personal information.

Also check:

Platform -> Settings -> System Health

Confirm database and Prisma connectivity.

## Pilot Support Notes

- Keep pilot businesses small and supported manually.
- Confirm each business can log in before handing over.
- Verify at least one customer, one program, one scan, one stamp, and one redemption per pilot business.
- Do not promise automated WhatsApp, SMS, email, wallet, or camera scanning during the pilot.
