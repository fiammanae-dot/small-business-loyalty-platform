# Pilot FAQ

## Business Owner Questions

### Can I create more branches?

Only up to your subscription plan limit.

### Can I create more loyalty programs?

Only up to your subscription plan limit.

### Are messages sent automatically?

No. Messages are prepared only. Manual sending is required during pilot.

### Can I edit branding?

Branding is managed by the System Administrator during pilot.

## Branch Manager Questions

### Can I create loyalty programs?

No. Business Owners create and edit programs.

### Can I redeem rewards?

Yes, Branch Managers can redeem eligible rewards.

### Can I see customers from other branches?

You can view customers belonging to the same business, while branch performance remains branch-specific.

## Staff Questions

### Can I redeem rewards?

No. Ask a Branch Manager or Business Owner.

### What if the camera does not work?

Use manual token entry on the scanner page.

### Why do I need a reason for multiple stamps?

Multiple stamps can trigger suspicious activity monitoring, so the reason is required.

## Customer Questions

### Do I need an app?

No. The customer card opens in the browser.

### Why is my phone number masked?

For privacy.

### How do I share referrals?

Open your card and use the referral section.

## Scanner Usage

### What if QR is invalid?

Confirm the customer is showing a LoyaltyBase card for the correct business.

### What if QR says wrong business?

The customer is showing a card from another business.

## Reward Redemption

### When is a reward ready?

When loyalty progress reaches the required stamps.

### What happens after redemption?

Earned stamps reset and bonus stamps are restored for the next cycle.

## Billing

### Can Business Owners pay online?

No. Pilot billing is manually tracked by System Administrator.

## Support

### How do I report an issue?

Send business name, user role, page, device, browser, and steps to reproduce to the pilot support contact.
