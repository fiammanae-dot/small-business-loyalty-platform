# Known Limitations

These limitations apply during the controlled pilot.

## Messaging

- No automatic WhatsApp sending yet.
- No automatic SMS sending yet.
- No automatic email sending yet.
- Messages are prepared manually only.
- Business users must copy and send messages themselves.
- Mark Sent Manually only records that a user confirmed manual sending.

## Scanner

- Camera scanner is not yet implemented.
- Staff and Branch Managers must paste a scan token or full scan URL.
- QR scanning validates the token and business ownership, but it does not use a live camera feed.

## Wallet

- Apple Wallet and Google Wallet buttons are placeholders or preparation-only.
- No real wallet pass generation is available during pilot.

## Payments

- Invoices and payments are tracked manually.
- No Stripe integration.
- No PayPal integration.
- No UAE payment gateway integration.
- No automatic payment collection.

## Notifications and Engagement

- Engagement events are generated and visible.
- Message preparation is available.
- Automated campaigns are not available.
- Birthday and inactive-customer events may require operational review and future scheduling support.

## Reporting

- CSV exports are available.
- PDF reports are not available.
- Advanced analytics are not available.
- Branch reporting is basic and should be reviewed manually.

## Operational Support

- Pilot businesses should be supported manually.
- Staff should be trained before using scanner and stamp issuance.
- Branch Managers should review reward redemption rules before pilot go-live.
- System Administrator should monitor health, alerts, subscriptions, and launch readiness regularly.

## Recommended Pilot Scope

Use Loyalty Card UAE with selected small businesses only.

Recommended pilot size:

- 1 to 5 businesses initially
- 1 to 3 branches per business
- Limited staff users
- Manual operational support during testing
