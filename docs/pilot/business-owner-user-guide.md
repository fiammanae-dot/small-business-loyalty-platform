# Business Owner User Guide

This guide explains how Business Owners use LoyaltyBase during the pilot.

## Dashboard Overview

Open Business after signing in.

The dashboard shows:

- Today activity
- Customer health
- Risk alerts
- Message queue status
- Onboarding checklist
- Business health
- Quick actions

Use the primary action buttons to quickly add customers, open scanner-related customer tools, manage programs, and review notifications.

## Create Customers

Open:

Business -> Customers -> Enroll customer

Enter customer details and marketing consent if applicable. LoyaltyBase uses phone normalization to avoid duplicate global customer records.

## Create Loyalty Programs

Open:

Business -> Programs -> Create Program

Set:

- Program name
- Product or service
- Required stamps
- Starting bonus stamps
- Reward name
- Reward description

Progress is calculated dynamically from earned stamps plus bonus stamps.

## Enroll Customers Into Programs

Open:

Business -> Programs -> Program -> Customers

Select an enrolled customer and add them to the program. The customer receives the program starting bonus stamps immediately.

## View Customer Cards

Open:

Business -> Customers -> Customer Profile

Use the Customer Card section to:

- Open the public card
- Copy the card link
- Share through WhatsApp manually
- Enable or disable the card

## Review Alerts

Open:

Business -> Notifications

Alerts help identify unusual stamp activity. Use:

- Investigate Alert
- View Customer
- View Staff
- View Activity

Mark alerts as Reviewed or Dismissed after investigation.

## Redeem Rewards

Rewards are redeemed from the scan result page when a customer is Reward Ready.

Business Owners and Branch Managers can redeem rewards. Staff cannot redeem rewards.

After redemption:

- A redemption audit record is created.
- Earned stamps reset to 0.
- Bonus stamps return to the program starting bonus.
- The customer begins a new loyalty cycle.

## Prepare Messages

Open:

Business -> Engagement -> Engagement Event

Use:

- Prepare WhatsApp Message
- Prepare SMS Message
- Prepare Email Message

Messages are prepared only. LoyaltyBase does not send messages automatically during the pilot.

Open:

Business -> Message Outbox

Use the outbox to view prepared messages, copy message text, copy WhatsApp links, mark messages as sent manually, or cancel them.

## Export CSV Reports

Open:

Business -> Dashboard -> Exports

Available CSV exports:

- Customer Export
- Program Export
- Redemption Export
- Alert Export

Use exports for pilot review and manual reporting.

## Pilot Notes

- Use the onboarding checklist to complete setup.
- Ask the System Administrator for subscription or plan changes.
- Report confusing screens or workflow gaps during the pilot.
