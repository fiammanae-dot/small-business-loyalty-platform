# Staff User Guide

This guide explains how Staff use LoyaltyBase during the pilot.

## Staff Dashboard

After signing in, Staff see:

- Staff name
- Business name
- Branch name
- Today's Activity
- Recent Scan Activity
- Quick Scanner button

## Enroll Customers

Open:

Staff -> Enroll customer

Enter the customer details. Staff enrollment assigns the customer to the staff member's branch as the card issue branch.

## Open Scanner

Open:

Staff -> Scanner

Paste the customer scan token or full scan URL.

## Scan QR

The scan result page shows whether the QR is valid for the business.

Status banners include:

- Valid Customer
- Reward Ready
- Suspicious Activity Alert
- Disabled Card
- Wrong Business
- Invalid Token

## Add Stamps

On a valid scan result, use Add Stamp.

Rules:

- Quantity can be 1 to 5.
- Reason is required for 2 to 5 stamps.
- Add a clear reason for multiple stamps, such as customer purchased multiple items.

## Why Staff Cannot Redeem Rewards

Reward redemption is restricted to Business Owners and Branch Managers.

This protects the business from accidental or unauthorized reward claims.

If a customer is Reward Ready, ask a Branch Manager or Business Owner to complete the redemption.

## Invalid or Wrong-Business QR

If QR is invalid:

1. Ask the customer to refresh or reopen their loyalty card.
2. Confirm they are showing the correct business card.
3. Call a Branch Manager if the issue continues.

Do not manually add stamps outside LoyaltyBase.
