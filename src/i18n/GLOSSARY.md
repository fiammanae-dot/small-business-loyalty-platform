# Arabic glossary (approved)

One Arabic term per English term, used everywhere in the product. Modern
Standard Arabic as written in the UAE; Western digits (0-9); dates with Arabic
month names; currency as "100 د.إ". Buttons use nouns (حفظ, not احفظ). The
platform-admin side (`/platform`) stays English.
